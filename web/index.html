Where do I find my Team ID?

On the FPL website, open the Points tab. Your Team ID is the number after /entry/ in the address bar, e.g. fantasy.premierleague.com/entry/1234567/event/4. You can paste the whole link.

Questions

Is Touchline free?

Yes. It's a closed alpha with no payments or subscriptions. Some deployments ask for a tester access code.

Do you need my FPL password?

Never. Touchline only reads the public squad behind your Team ID — the same data anyone can see on the FPL website. Your squad is saved in your own browser.

Where do the projections come from?

From a projection model that is frozen into a release before it reaches the app, so the same squad always gets the same answer. Until a release is approved for production it is labelled “Model preview”.

Are the recommendations guaranteed?

No. Football is unpredictable and projections can be wrong. Touchline shows its uncertainty and flags fragile decisions, but the decision is always yours.

Which Gameweeks are covered?

Whatever the current release publishes — up to five Gameweeks ahead. Longer horizons appear automatically when they are released.
