How chips are scored

Pick a chip in the “Your team” panel to try it in the first published Gameweek, and mark chips you have already played this half-season as used. A chip is always a what-if: your squad rating stays chip-free.

  • Bench Boost adds all four bench players' projected points.
  • Triple Captain counts your captain's projected points three times.
  • Wildcard makes staged moves free, keeps your saved free transfers, and keeps the new squad.
  • Free Hit makes staged moves free for one Gameweek; later Gameweeks use your committed squad.

Touchline scores the chip you choose. It does not tell you which week to play it.
