01What the three model fixes changed

Each was a case of "wait until GW3 for a form-informed model" that the model still couldn't act on. All three now carry visible flags.

Empty-history players are no longer dead weight

A player with an all-zero previous-season workbook row (promoted, new signing, returning) was blended toward a hard 0.0 start probability at ~62% weight. Tzolis went 0.38 → 0.80 start probability, Jacquet 0.38 → 0.80 — both had started every game. Their 5-GW projections roughly doubled (Tzolis 6.5 → 15.0). They are now "playing but low-ceiling", which is the honest read.

Deadline-day signings are flagged as rotation risks

The model rated Enzo and Ndiaye at 0.98–0.99 start probability on the strength of their old club's 35+ starts — both signed for City on 1 Sep and have one City start each. Now 0.67, carrying CHANGED_CLUBS_SINCE_SEASON_START. This matches your read: adding Enzo and Ndiaye to a forward line that already rotates Doku / Semenyo / Foden / Cherki is a genuine minutes risk. A settled summer signing (Rogers, Mbeumo, 3/3 starts) keeps full weight.

Team strength now sees the first three Gameweeks

Every defensive projection was on the preseason team prior — the model still thought Spurs had a top-four defence. It now blends toward the season's own team-level xG for and against (five-match pseudo-count). Spurs' defensive-weakness ratio moved 0.89 → 1.01 (top-four → league average), Arsenal 0.60 → 0.45 (elite, confirmed). Van Hecke fell from ~24 to 18.9 over five GW. With only three games the shift is partial — trust your own read of Spurs over the residual.

The one call to reconsider

Dropping B.Fernandes entirely costs ~17 raw points over five Gameweeks — his 34.3 is the single highest midfielder on the board and Man Utd's GW4–8 run (MCI, then FUL / TOT / LEE / BOU) is soft. "United not that good" is a team-quality read; the model is pricing his individual rate and the fixtures, and both are strong. Keeping him + Mbeumo and spreading cheaper elsewhere loses you far less than dropping him does.

05If you use the 3 free transfers — on your actual squad

The model's own 3-FT plan opens Isak → Thiago + Maguire → Guéhi in GW4. Overriding the Isak call:

  • OUT: Tzolis (15.0, a fringe/differential at best) and Jacquet (11.6, no attacking threat, Liverpool CB depth). The two the model is unambiguously right about.
  • IN: a nailed, no-Europe mid — Tavernier (BOU, 23.6), Stach (LEE, 20.7), or Groß (BHA, 19.7) — and a nailed cheap defender — Guéhi (MCI, 24.9), Tarkowski (EVE, 21.8), or Thiaw (NEW, 20.4). Both are clear upgrades on what leaves.
  • Third transfer: hold it. The model spends it on Maguire → a defender or Gakpo → a mid; neither is urgent, and you're back to 3 FT next week. Keeps flexibility for team news.
  • Keep Isak. The model's Isak → Thiago is driven by the start-probability blind spot, not his football — with Ekitike out he is nailed.
  • Never start Dubravka. A £4.0 non-playing GK2 is the correct structure (Raya + dead keeper beats Raya + a £4.5 backup, per the set-and-forget check) — just never field him.

That is +30–35 xPts over five Gameweeks from two moves, no hit, Wildcard and the third transfer both still in hand.
